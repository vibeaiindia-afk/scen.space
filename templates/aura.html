01 · Design

Made of one piece.

A single block of titanium, machined until the seam disappears. Lighter in the hand than it looks in a photograph, and cold for about a second before it warms to you.

See finishes
02 · Camera

Reads light first.

Four lenses that agree with each other. It meters the room before the shutter moves, so a dim restaurant looks like the evening you remember rather than the noise a sensor saw.

Sample gallery
03 · Performance

Quiet, then fast.

The chip idles at almost nothing and steps up only when you ask. Editing, rendering, a game with everything turned on — and the back of the phone stays a temperature you would put in a pocket.

Full specification
04 · Battery

Ends the day with you.

Two days of ordinary use, and half a charge in twenty minutes when the day turns out not to be ordinary. Charge it overnight if you like the ritual; you will not need to.
